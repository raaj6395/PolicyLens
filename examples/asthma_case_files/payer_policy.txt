Specialty Drug Policy RX-58: Dupilumab for severe asthma (synthetic)

Dupilumab is medically necessary for severe asthma in patients 12 years or older when ALL of the following are met: (1) eosinophilic phenotype, shown by EITHER a blood eosinophil count of 150 cells/uL or greater within the past 12 months, OR a FeNO of 25 ppb or greater within the past 12 months; (2) at least 2 asthma exacerbations requiring systemic corticosteroids in the past 12 months, or at least 1 requiring hospitalization; (3) adherence to high-dose inhaled corticosteroid plus a second controller (LABA, LAMA or leukotriene antagonist) for at least 3 consecutive months before the request; (4) pre-bronchodilator FEV1 below 80% predicted within the past 12 months; (5) the patient is not a current smoker; (6) dupilumab will not be used in combination with another biologic for asthma; (7) prescribed by or in consultation with a pulmonologist or allergist.
